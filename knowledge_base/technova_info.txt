## 1. Company Overview
TechNova Solutions is a cloud-based business software company founded in 2018 and headquartered in Austin, Texas. We help small and mid-sized businesses streamline operations, manage customer relationships, and unlock data-driven insights through our integrated software suite.

Our mission: "Empowering businesses with intelligent technology that grows with them."

## 2. Products & Services

### A. TechNova CRM Suite
Our flagship CRM platform helps sales and support teams manage leads, track deals, and close more revenue.
Key Features:
- Contact & account management
- Sales pipeline visualization
- Email integration (Gmail, Outlook)
- Automated follow-up sequences
- Real-time reporting & dashboards
- Mobile app for iOS and Android

### B. TechNova ERP Pro
An integrated enterprise resource planning system connecting finance, inventory, HR, and operations.
Key Features:
- Financial accounting and invoicing
- Inventory management with barcode scanning
- HR & payroll processing
- Purchase orders and vendor management
- Multi-location warehouse support
- Compliance and audit trail

### C. TechNova Analytics Hub
A business intelligence and reporting platform that transforms raw data into actionable insights.
Key Features:
- Drag-and-drop dashboard builder
- 150+ pre-built report templates
- Real-time KPI monitoring
- Predictive sales forecasting (AI-powered)
- Integrates with CRM, ERP, and third-party data sources
- Shareable reports with role-based access

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I migrate data from my existing CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot)?
A: Yes. We offer free data migration assistance for Professional and Enterprise plan customers. Our team handles CSV imports, API-based migrations, and field mapping.

Q: Does TechNova integrate with QuickBooks or Xero?
A: Yes. TechNova ERP Pro includes native integrations with QuickBooks Online, Xero, and Zoho Books. Additional integrations available via our REST API.

Q: Is there a mobile app?
A: Yes. TechNova CRM has iOS and Android apps. ERP mobile access is available via browser on all plans.

Q: What happens after my trial ends?
A: You'll receive a reminder email 3 days before your trial ends. Your data is preserved for 30 days after expiry, giving you time to choose a plan.

Q: Can I add more users mid-subscription?
A: Yes. Additional user seats can be added at any time and are billed on a pro-rated basis.
